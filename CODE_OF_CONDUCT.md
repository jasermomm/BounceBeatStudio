# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the project.

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, dox, or publish private information.
- Keep issue and pull-request discussions relevant.
- Respect licenses and ownership of MIDI, SoundFonts, images, and other media.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove disruptive content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
